## Decisions and why

1. **One typed-failure contract** (W1, `modules/design/app/compose/errors.ts`, documented in `BACKEND-CONTRACT.md`).
   - Every Design rejection maps to a `DesignFailure` (kind, outcome, recovery, section, operation). `designFeedback` turns it into toast and inline props with a stable id and a status word.
   - `write` is required in every context. An unrecognised failure is `unknown` for a write and `unavailable` for a read.
   - Only `unknown` carries an unknown effect, and it offers **Check**, never a blind Retry.
   - The wording matches Work ("not confirmed yet", **Unconfirmed**, Check) and the shell ("Unavailable").
   - A storage outage shares the toast id `design.storage.unavailable`, so many sections failing show one toast.
2. **One freshness mechanism: project content events.** Writers notify after any attempt to write (success, refusal or unknown), and readers re-read. F4's `activeProject` and `readContent` stay in F4's lane. W3's hook plus `contentFor(project, { revision })` is the documented path for views.
3. **Contract layout.**
   - W1 owns §1–§6: failures, envelopes, events, destinations, capabilities and the current project.
   - W4 adds §2a, Taste.
   - W3 adds §7–§11: fresh content, annotation selection, capture thumbnails, operation names, Compare.
   - W2 adds §12, Imports.
   - W5 replaces §5 and adds an export section.
4. **Enablement governance.** Imports enablement (W2) and export enablement (W5's last commit) both fall under `ac.import-export-enablement.001` in `docs/engineering-specs/hq-integration-parallel-execution.md`.
   - The import-export qualification suites (crash, CAS and privacy) must pass at one exact head, and the integration owner (T1 in that spec) accepts that evidence.
   - The spec says no further owner approval is needed.
   - Both PR bodies must say this and carry the suite counts at their exact head: the `hq-design-storage-engine` and `hq-shared-r3` groups in `tests/check-groups.toml`.
   - The code's own export gate (`EXPORT_STATUS` in `modules/design/app/storage/export.ts`) says export stays disabled until the privacy projection passes conformance.
5. **W5 is backend-only.** It covers the capabilities route, the renderer ports and the gated enablement commit. F4 and F5 decide where an export action lives. The Imports panel's backup over the bridge stays unavailable, because the bridge has no route that lists blobs.
6. **Views.** B3 touches F4 and F5 views for wiring only. The one exception is a minimal, unstyled typed notice beside the Taste panel (W4), which F5 restyles.
